# Content Lifecycle

## States
1. **Draft**: Content being created, only visible to authors and editors.
2. **Review**: Content submitted for approval.
3. **Published**: Statically generated and available on the public site.
4. **Archived**: Removed from active view but retained in the institutional archive (never deleted without policy).

## Governance
- AI-generated content is never automatically considered institutional truth; requires human review.

## DECISION REQUIRED
- Who holds the final approval rights for publishing major institutional content?
